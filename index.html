07 / STATISTICAL INFERENCE

HYPOTHESIS TESTING

Inferential statistical evaluations conducted at the 5% significance level (α = 0.05).

TEST 01 • SCIPY

Paired t-Test

Male vs Female Adolescent Mortality (1990–2023)

H₀ (Null): Mean yearly male and female rates are equal (μ₁ = μ₂)
H₁ (Alt): Mean yearly male and female rates differ (μ₁ ≠ μ₂)
t-STATISTIC

9.5939

p-VALUE

4.525e-11

DECISION (p < 0.05) REJECT H₀
Conclusion: Statistically significant evidence that male adolescent mortality is substantially higher than female mortality in Sri Lanka.
TEST 02 • SCIPY

Pearson Correlation

Adolescent Mortality Rate vs Year (Both Sexes)

H₀ (Null): No linear correlation between year & rate (ρ = 0)
H₁ (Alt): A linear correlation exists between year & rate (ρ ≠ 0)
CORRELATION (r)

-0.7758

p-VALUE

4.386e-08

DECISION (p < 0.05) REJECT H₀
Conclusion: Strong negative linear relationship (r = -0.78), showing a significant downward trend in adolescent mortality over time.
INFERENTIAL STATISTICS • PYTHON SCIPY.STATS • SIGNIFICANCE LEVEL α = 0.05
